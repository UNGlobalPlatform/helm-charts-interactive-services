**Before you start**
- Onyxia suspends this service 13 hours after it was launched or last resumed,
  whether or not you are using it. Resume it from *My Services*; your data is kept.
{{- if $deleted }}
- ⚠️ A service left suspended for 30 days is deleted automatically, with
  {{ $deleted }}. Resume it, or download what you need, before then.
- ⚠️ Deleting this service permanently deletes {{ $deleted }}.
{{- if eq .Values.objectStorage.provider "s3" }}
  Your files stay in your S3 bucket, but a new service won't recognise them as datasets.
{{- end }}
{{- end }}
{{- if eq .Values.objectStorage.provider "s3" }}
- S3 access expires about 11 hours after launch and isn't renewed when the
  service resumes. Use MinIO (the default) for work that lasts longer than a day.
{{- end }}
- Existing Trade Data Tool 2.x services keep running and resume as they are;
  they can't be upgraded to {{ .Chart.AppVersion }}. Your datasets, metadata, rules and
  users don't carry over: set them up again in this service, then import your files.
  Keep the old service until you have downloaded what you need.

**What's new in 3.x** ([full notes](https://github.com/UNGlobalPlatform/helm-charts-interactive-services/blob/main/charts/trade-data-tool/README.md#whats-new-in-3x))
- **Dates default to day-first** when you import a file (new *Date/Time Format*
  option; *Regional Format* is now *Decimal Format*).
- **Data Search** (formerly *Advance Search*): faster paging, saved searches, and
  *Download All* for large downloads, collected from the new *My Downloads* page.
- **Pipeline**: *Retry from step*, *Reset* and *Delete batch* in the background,
  and a new *Constraint* data-quality stage.
- **Imports**: JSON files, and reusable column-mapping presets.
- The *Bronze / Silver / Gold* menu links are gone: open data from a dataset's
  *View Data* action.
- You're signed out after 60 minutes of inactivity.
